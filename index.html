The world, one port at a time

Follow along as we explore the world together — one port, one adventure at a time. From Caribbean beaches to Norwegian fjords, Pacific islands to European rivers, South American glaciers to Asian harbors, we’ve been fortunate to experience so many incredible places and cultures through our shared love of cruising. This map is a visual diary of our travels, a way to relive the memories and share the stories behind each port we’ve visited. Whether you’re an avid cruiser or just love armchair travel, this is our journey.
